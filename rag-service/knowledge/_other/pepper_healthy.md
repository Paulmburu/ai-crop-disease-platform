# Healthy bell pepper leaves

## Overview
A healthy bell pepper leaf is uniformly green, smooth and glossy, without spots, blisters, yellow halos, curling or mottling.

## What to keep doing
- Water at the base (drip) rather than over the leaves, and avoid working among wet plants.
- Rotate crops so peppers and tomatoes are not grown in the same place every year.
- Use certified seed and inspect transplants before planting.
- Scout weekly, checking both leaf surfaces, especially after warm, rainy weather when bacterial spot spreads fastest.

## When to look closer
Small water-soaked spots on the leaf underside, sudden leaf drop, or raised scabby spots on fruit suggest bacterial spot. A healthy classification from one photo is not a guarantee: early infections can be invisible.
