# Healthy tomato leaves

## Overview
A healthy tomato leaf is evenly green without spots, rings, yellow halos, mould on the underside, curling, mottling or stippling.

## What to keep doing
- Stake and prune for airflow; mulch to keep soil off the leaves.
- Water at the base rather than over the plant.
- Rotate tomatoes, potatoes and peppers with unrelated crops.
- Wash hands and tools before handling plants (mosaic viruses spread on hands).
- Scout weekly, including the leaf underside for mites and whiteflies.

## When to look closer
Dark ringed spots (early blight, target spot), tiny spots with black dots (Septoria), greasy dark blotches (late blight), yellow spots with mould underneath (leaf mold), upward curling and yellowing (TYLCV), or mottling (mosaic). A single healthy-looking photo cannot rule out early infection.
