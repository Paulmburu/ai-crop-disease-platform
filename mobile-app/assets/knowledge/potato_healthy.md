# Healthy potato leaves

## Overview
A healthy potato leaf is evenly green without spots, target-like rings, water-soaked patches, white fuzzy growth or yellowing.

## What to keep doing
- Plant certified seed tubers and remove volunteer potatoes and cull piles.
- Rotate potatoes and tomatoes with unrelated crops.
- Keep plants vigorous with balanced nutrition and even watering.
- Scout at least weekly, more often in cool, humid weather when late blight can spread within days.

## When to look closer
Dark spots with concentric rings on older leaves suggest early blight; fast-spreading greasy, dark lesions with white growth underneath suggest late blight. A single healthy-looking photo cannot rule out early infection.
